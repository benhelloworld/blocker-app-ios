# App Store Notes

## Positioning

Good:
- "Stay committed to your focus plan"
- "Reduce impulse app switching"
- "Built with Apple's Screen Time framework"
- "Make distracting apps harder to access during focus blocks"

Avoid:
- "Impossible to bypass"
- "Unbreakable"
- "Locks your phone permanently"
- Any claim implying unauthorized device control

## Review notes draft

This app uses Apple's Screen Time APIs to help users voluntarily configure focus schedules and shield selected apps/websites during those schedules. Users grant Family Controls permission and choose what to include.
